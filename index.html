How I work

Physics first, across fields

Trained as a physicist, I go to the most fundamental level to find a solution, verify it, and push it further:

Core topics

Topic 1

Resonators

High-Q photonic-crystal cavities in InP and silicon — design through rigorous FDTD simulation, Q-factor analysis, and fiber-coupling optimization.

OE 2009OE 2010JOSA B 2012 · ALD & Q
Topic 2

Lasers

III–V quantum-well nanolasers fabricated and characterized end to end — spectra, L–L, threshold, linewidth. Demonstrated low-threshold continuous-wave lasing from a nanobeam printed on SiO₂/Si, with thermal management built into the design.

OE 2014 · CW nanobeam laserOE 2009 · 650 µW threshold
Topic 3

Fiber coupling

Evanescent coupling between cavities, waveguides, and fibers — worked out in theory, modeled in in-house FDTD, and built on the bench. A single-mode fiber tapered to ~1 µm and curved into a probe delivers the pump, extracts photons (~80% predicted), and tunes the resonance.

OE 2009JOSA B 2012 · theoryLaser & Photonics Rev. 2011 · cover
Topic 4

Silicon integration

Heterogeneous III–V-on-silicon: dry transfer printing of nanolasers and single nanowires onto silicon photonic circuits, with FDTD-guided coupling into the silicon waveguide.

OE 2014iNOW 2012
