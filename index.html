About

I'm a photonics and optical-systems engineer who works across the full development cycle — device physics and simulation, nanofabrication, optical characterization, system validation, and hardware integration. My career has moved from the smallest optical resonators to the systems that put light to work at scale.

Today I lead end-to-end optical performance modeling and validation for AR/VR displays at Apple, informing architecture and hardware decisions across XR optical stacks. Along the way I've built time-of-flight optical brain-sensing systems, custom high-speed two-photon microscopes for recording live neural activity, and heterogeneously integrated nanolasers on silicon.

Experience

    AR/VR Optical Module

    Optical Scientist Meta — Facebook Reality Labs

    Optical Brain–Computer Interface

    Developed a multi-channel time-of-flight diffuse optical sensing system — fiber arrays, pulsed lasers, photon-counting detectors, and time-taggers — for deep-tissue brain-activity recording. Led system validation across SNR, dynamic range, timing jitter, and impulse response.

    Facebook Reality Labs presents the time-of-flight optical brain–computer interface program (talk jumps to the relevant segment). Watch on YouTube ↗

  3. 2013 — 2019

    Research Scientist & Postdoctoral Researcher Stanford University — Neuroscience

    Computational Vision · Multi-Photon Retinal Imaging (Baccus Lab)

    Designed and built a custom high-speed multi-photon imaging system — ultrafast lasers, laser scanner, electro-optic modulator, precision fiber coupling — for large-scale neural recording in live retina. Developed automated image-processing pipelines and interpretable CNN models to characterize nonlinear retinal information processing.

  4. 2011 — 2012

    Postdoctoral Researcher KAIST — Mechanical Engineering

    Silicon Photonics Integration (Bumki Min Lab)

    Developed heterogeneous integration of III–V nanolasers onto silicon photonic integrated circuits via dry transfer printing, demonstrating direct coupling into silicon waveguides and continuous-wave lasing on silica-on-silicon platforms.

  5. 2009

    Visiting Researcher University of Arizona — Optical Sciences

    Silicon Optical Resonators (Hyatt Gibbs Lab, QNOS)

    Built a fiber-coupled optical spectroscopy system for silicon high-Q photonic-crystal nano-resonators in the telecom band.

  6. 2007 — 2011

    PhD Researcher KAIST — Physics

    Photonic Crystal Resonators & Fiber Coupling (Yong-Hee Lee Lab)

    Demonstrated microfiber-based optical I/O for InP photonic-crystal resonators at telecom wavelengths, developed 3D FDTD models of evanescent coupling, and fabricated III–V and silicon nanophotonic devices using e-beam lithography, CAIBE, RIE, and wet etching.
